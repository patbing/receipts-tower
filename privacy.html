Privacy

Receipts Tower is a static page. It has no server and no analytics.

  • Your Pollinations key is kept in this tab's session storage until you close the tab or press Disconnect. It is sent only to gen.pollinations.ai, in an Authorization header.
  • Jobs, decisions and rental applications are saved in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded; the application file is downloaded to your computer.
  • Live jobs send the company brief and the earlier agents' notes to Pollinations and are paid from your own Pollen.
  • The 3D view loads three.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
